# Frequently asked questions

## Can I deposit real money now?

No fund has a verified deployment that accepts real money yet. Connecting a wallet, reading risks, or completing a design does not open deposits.

## Can shares be transferred?

Shares are currently nontransferable. They record your part of the fund and your right to withdraw. Share allowances permit designated withdrawal uses, not share transfers.

## Is there a 90-day lock?

There is no fixed time lock. The creator seeds the fund with 100 accounting-asset units and pays a 1% creation fee on that seed, so the standard net contribution is 99 units. The seed pays no deposit fee on top. While other holders remain, the creator must keep at least 2% of circulating shares after withdrawing. Liquidity, protocol restrictions, and fees still affect withdrawals.

## Do I sign every trade after depositing?

No. The fund's plan or AI agent acts autonomously within the mandate. Holders do not sign each trade. Your own deposits, withdrawals, and necessary approvals still require wallet confirmation.

## Who can replace the AI agent?

The creator may disable the AI agent immediately through governance. Replacement requires an announcement and a wait of at least 24 hours. An individual holder cannot replace the fund's AI agent.

## How are fund fees calculated?

- **Creation fee:** 1% of the creator's seed, charged once when the fund goes live. Only the creator pays it, to CRE8.
- **Deposit fee:** 1% of every deposit except the seed, including the creator's later deposits. It goes to CRE8. It is charged on the gross amount actually used; unused budget is refunded.
- **Performance fee:** 10% of profit for CRE8 (fixed) plus 0–20% for the creator (fixed at creation; default 10%), at most 30% in total. It is charged only on your own realized profit when you withdraw, based on your own cost basis. No profit, no fee. The screen shows it as one total, for example "Performance fee 25% (CRE8 10% + creator 15%)".

There is no annual, management, operation, or withdrawal fee. DEX fees and slippage go to the DEX, not to CRE8. See [Fees](../product/fees.md).

## Does AI agent loss protection limit my maximum loss?

No. AI agent loss protection (a threshold of 2–10%, default 5%) and the 5% idle requirement are different mechanisms. An action that crosses the threshold completes before the AI agent is disabled, and markets and other risks can cause greater losses. See [Risk controls](../product/risk-controls.md).

## What are AI credits for?

CRE8 AI questions, comparisons, designs, and plan reviews. Credits cannot be transferred or withdrawn and do not cover fund fees or gas. See [CRE8 AI and credits](../guides/cre8-ai-credits.md) for draft prices, post-delivery charges, and revisions.

## Can I withdraw without AI credits?

Direct withdrawals and authority management do not depend on credits. They still require valid fund, deployment, liquidity, and eligibility conditions. Routine execution of a configured plan also does not consume chat credits.

## My purchased credits are missing. Should I pay again?

Check the original order and transaction, wait for final confirmation, or request reconciliation. A timeout does not prove failure. Do not pay again.

## Does withdrawing as assets always succeed?

No. You may receive protocol receipts. Moving them and withdrawing from the underlying protocol later are still subject to that protocol's restrictions. See [Withdrawals](../guides/redemptions.md).

## Is interface performance live track record?

Current examples are simulated. Performance figures, valuations, and previews do not replace live history or your actual net results. See [Share price and performance](../product/nav-and-performance.md).
