# Frequently asked questions

## Can I deposit live funds now?

No verified vault deployment accepts live funds. Connecting a wallet, reading risks, or completing a design does not open deposits.

## Can shares be transferred?

Shares are currently nontransferable and record participation and redemption rights. Share allowances permit designated exit uses, not share transfers.

## Is there a 90-day lock?

There is no fixed time lock. Creators contribute 100 accounting-asset units and pay the entry fee. While others remain, they must retain at least 2% of circulating shares after redemption. Liquidity, protocol restrictions, and fees still affect exits.

## Do I sign every trade after depositing?

The Operator acts autonomously within the mandate without per-trade depositor signatures. Your own deposits, exits, and necessary approvals still require wallet confirmation.

## Who can replace the execution agent?

The creator may disable the agent immediately through governance. Replacement requires announcement and at least 24 hours. An individual depositor cannot replace the entire vault's execution agent.

## How are vault fees calculated?

The entry fee is 0.5%. Applicable fund operations pay 0.1%, subject to a fixed annual-period cap. Positive exit profit pays platform 10% plus creator 0–20%, using that address's cost basis for this exit. No positive profit means no performance fee for that exit. See [Fees](../product/fees.md).

## Does the 5% protection guarantee a maximum loss?

No. Agent-operation loss protection and 5% idle requirements are different. A threshold-crossing action completes before disabling the agent, and markets and other risks can cause greater losses. See [Risk controls](../product/risk-controls.md).

## What are AI credits for?

CRE8 AI questions, comparisons, designs, and strategy reviews. Credits cannot be transferred or withdrawn and exclude vault fees and gas. See [CRE8 AI and credits](../guides/cre8-ai-credits.md) for draft prices, post-delivery charges, and revisions.

## Can I exit without AI credits?

Direct exits and authority management do not depend on credits, but still require valid vault, deployment, liquidity, and eligibility conditions. Routine execution of configured strategies also does not consume chat credits.

## My purchased credits are missing. Should I pay again?

Check the original order and transaction, wait for final confirmation, or request reconciliation. A timeout does not prove failure. Do not pay again.

## Does in-kind redemption always succeed?

No. You may receive receipts subject to transfer and redemption restrictions at the underlying protocol. See [Redemptions and exits](../guides/redemptions.md).

## Is interface performance live track record?

Current examples are simulated. APY, valuation, and previews do not replace live history or your actual net results. See [NAV and performance](../product/nav-and-performance.md).
