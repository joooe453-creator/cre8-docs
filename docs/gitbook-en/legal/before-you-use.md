# Before you use CRE8

Version: 2026-10-07-draft.3. Draft product summary; full legal documents retain their own version.

CRE8 remains a prototype without a verified vault deployment accepting live funds. Example holdings and performance are simulated. The service entity and open markets are not finalized.

## Before participating

**You may lose your entire principal.** Markets, depegs, contracts, data, third parties, and liquidity involve risk. Principal, returns, and exit timing are not guaranteed.

**The Operator may act autonomously.** After deposit it may operate within the mandate without your signature on every trade. Execution-agent replacement has a governance delay.

**Fees are calculated separately.** Entry is 0.5%; applicable fund operations are 0.1%, subject to a fixed annual-period cap. Positive exit profit pays platform 10% plus creator 0–20%, based on that address's cost basis for the exit. AI credits, gas, slippage, and third-party costs are separate. See [Fees](../product/fees.md).

**No fixed time lock does not guarantee liquidity.** Creators contribute 100 units and retain at least 2% of circulating shares while others remain. Shares are nontransferable. In-kind exits may deliver restricted receipts.

**Risk controls do not guarantee a maximum loss.** Agent-operation loss protection at 5% and the 5% idle requirement have distinct conditions. A crossing action completes before the agent is disabled; other market losses may be greater.

**Review AI output.** Sign-in, buying credits, and receiving a proposal do not grant authority over funds. No delivery means no credit charge; revisions are charged separately by usage. Prices remain drafts. See [CRE8 AI and credits](../guides/cre8-ai-credits.md).

## Reading and signing

Read the selected vault's Fund prospectus, including assets, strategy, fees, permissions, and exit conditions. General risk notices, reading prompts, legally required acceptance, token approvals, and transactions are distinct. Dismissing a notice does not establish eligibility or acceptance of a live transaction.

Before signing, check network, vault, assets, amount, recipient, spender and allowance, minimum shares or proceeds, and deadline. For unknown results, query the original transaction instead of sending again. Never disclose private keys or seed phrases.
