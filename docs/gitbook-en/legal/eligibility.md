# CRE8 draft eligibility and jurisdiction restrictions

Version: 2026-10-07-draft.8. Status: for product and legal finalization; not in effect or deployed.

This page adapts the public draft of that version for product reading. It is not the identical file in the original legal bundle or an effective agreement.

The entity, registered jurisdiction, operating location, and initial markets are undecided. Only a prototype exists, with no verified live deployment accepting funds. This document does not state that IP blocking, KYC, sanctions screening, or contract admission controls are implemented. The measures below are requirements awaiting implementation.

## I. Provisions for users to read

> This is candidate text for live service. It can become effective only after the entity, markets, features, notice, and contact arrangements are confirmed.

### 1. Eligibility

You must meet the legal age and capacity requirements where you are located to accept terms and use the feature. Acting for an entity or another person requires valid authority. You, the actual asset owner, and those represented must satisfy applicable market admission, identity, and investor qualifications.

Eligibility depends on the specific service, location and usual residence, entity incorporation and operations, and other conditions required by law. One enabled feature does not open every vault, asset, creator service, or token activity. Browsing, connecting a wallet, or calling a public contract does not establish qualification or regulatory permission.

### 2. Markets not yet open and restricted persons

Features are offered only in explicitly published open markets to eligible persons. Absence from an excluded list does not qualify a market missing from the open list. Proposed markets not initially open and their scope are in Part II. The final list follows completed legal assessment.

The following persons must not use restricted features, use them through others, or receive their benefits:

1. Persons lacking published admission or necessary qualifications for the feature.
2. Persons, entities, groups, or other bodies prohibited from receiving the service or transacting under applicable law, sanctions, courts, or regulatory measures.
3. Persons restricted through direct or indirect ownership, control, agency, or beneficiary relationships under applicable sanctions, even if not individually listed.
4. Persons acting, holding, or managing assets for those restricted persons, or giving them prohibited service or benefits indirectly.

Restrictions follow the applicable sanctions regime, effective designations, and transaction prohibitions rather than a fixed country list. Sanctions affecting a country do not automatically list every resident or ban every activity. Restrictions, exceptions, and licenses depend on applicable law.

### 3. No evasion or impersonation

Do not evade applicable limits through VPNs, proxies, false or concealed identity/location, others' accounts, nominee arrangements, or other means. Do not provide false information or assist restricted persons. Material changes in qualification, authority, or relevant identity should be reported through published contact channels.

### 4. Verification and service restrictions

Applicable law and published policy may require information, verification, or clarification before a feature is provided. Data uses, retention, and rights channels should appear in the privacy notice. Incomplete verification, inconsistencies, or possible matches may suspend new-risk features pending review. Wallet signatures, risk confirmations, or a single IP signal do not replace required checks.

Once restriction is confirmed, we may refuse or limit our interfaces, APIs, transaction preparation, or agent services within lawful and actual control. Relevant third-party restrictions should be disclosed before use. Their terms do not remove responsibility for our own service.

### 5. Existing positions, queries, and remedies

Restriction of new services is distinct from existing-position handling. For existing positions, explain lawful queries, exits, reviews, or regulatory-license routes according to law, contract capability, and third-party limits. Refusing new commercial terms alone must not hide legally available information or remedies.

Exits or refunds are not guaranteed immediate. Sanctions, court orders, freezes, or other restrictions may prohibit transfers, trades, or returns and require separate lawful handling. They do not automatically extinguish asset rights or permit us to confiscate funds. Public-chain or third-party technical limits do not exclude lawful complaints and remedies.

### 6. Platform obligations

Your declarations and consent do not replace the qualifications, permits, verification, screening, disclosure, asset-handling, or reporting duties of the platform, creators, or actual providers. This document does not exclude non-excludable liability, consumer rights, or regulatory complaints.

The full jurisdiction policy remains in Docs and the footer. The vault list and individual pages show depositor notices; Create shows creator notices. The same browser and wallet may hide that notice version. Subsequent page operations do not repeat general notices, and no jurisdiction or representative-authority checkbox is required there. Pressing Escape instead of “I understand the risks” returns to the previous page, or home if there is none, without recording acceptance. “I understand the risks,” “Don’t show this again,” and the Fund prospectus prompt's “Yes, continue” do not prove eligibility or location. Required identity, authority, beneficiary, jurisdiction, or investor information must be separately collected and verified under actual admission policy. No checkbox does not mean no requirement.

Public documents can be read without a wallet. Where law limits promotion or distribution, availability of those materials must be designed accordingly.

## II. Proposed market admission policy

**This is a commercial admission proposal awaiting confirmation, not a claim that all DeFi activities in these markets are legally banned. No live market has yet been selected.**

The proposal initially treats the United States, Canada, and mainland China as markets not open to CRE8's own services, restricting vault creation, new funds, increased investment risk, and corresponding API/agent services. Final scope requires approval after the entity, operating location, and initial markets are chosen. Existing exits follow Part I, section 5.

| Candidate market | Proposed admission scope | Required before finalization |
|---|---|---|
| United States | Individuals located or usually resident there; entities organized under U.S. law or primarily operating there are candidates for exclusion. Other U.S. connections are not automatically approved before assessment | Decide whether to include citizens, permanent residents, tax residents, or other applicable U.S. person definitions; identify the basis and feature without mixing legal definitions |
| Canada | Individuals located or usually resident there; entities incorporated or primarily operating there are candidates for exclusion | Provincial/territorial rules and classification of vaults, management, and promotion; decide other identity conditions rather than assuming all overseas Canadians are restricted by nationality |
| Mainland China | Individuals located or usually resident there; entities incorporated or primarily operating there are candidates for exclusion | Services directed to domestic users, domestic team/Operator activity, and cross-border conduct; Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan require separate assessment |

Location, residence, incorporation, and operations are proposed policy criteria. Live definitions must be definite and verifiable. User nationality and founder nationality are different and neither is a universal admission test.

Stock and RWA assets require individual review of actual rights, issuers, holding, transfers, management, and redemption qualifications. A general service list does not open them automatically.
