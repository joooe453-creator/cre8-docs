# CRE8 draft privacy notice

Version: 2026-10-07-draft.8. Status: not in effect. The live operator, controller, contact, providers, and markets remain undecided. The prototype accepts no live vault deposits, and this document does not enable personal-agent payments.

This page adapts the public draft of that version for product reading. It is not the identical file in the original legal bundle or an effective agreement.

## I. Scope

This draft describes processing when browsing CRE8, designing vaults, connecting wallets, preparing transactions, using general risk and Fund prospectus prompts, and choosing personal-agent sign-in, conversations, tasks, holdings sharing, credits, or payments. The public mobile chat preview differs from the live service interface: it does not call AI, take payment, or submit transactions. Server and AI data flows below occur when you actually use the corresponding features.

The live notice must identify the actual responsible entity, contacts, providers, and rights channels. Reading is notice, not consent to every purpose or authority to trade, withdraw, or operate a vault.

## II. Websites, wallets, and temporary page data

- **Website requests.** Websites, hosting, CDNs, and network services may receive IP, browser/device data, request paths, and times to deliver pages, maintain security, and troubleshoot. IP may indicate approximate location but does not reliably prove residence, nationality, or eligibility.
- **Wallets and onchain queries.** Connection or preparation reads the public address you provide, network, balances, shares, allowances, contract state, and history to display information, verify configuration, estimate/simulate, and check results. The platform does not request keys or seed phrases.
- **Design and transaction contents.** Name, Operator, assets/markets, caps, rates, amount, recipient, minimum proceeds, and deadline. Submitted transactions may remain public onchain. Unsubmitted local designs are saved through browser download, not automatically uploaded by downloading.
- **Local and workflow data.** The browser may save simulations and pending transactions. Risk and reading prompts address the current page flow, with temporary state that is not uploaded or server evidence of reading or legal acceptance. Returning to the page shows the notice again unless you check “Don’t show this again” and press “I understand the risks” (or “Got it” for the AI notice). Only then does the browser save the notice version and the last connected address; without a wallet it is recorded for that browser. It is used only to suppress that version, is not uploaded, and can be removed by clearing site data. There is no Close or X. Escape returns to the previous page, or home if there is none, without creating this persistent record. Checking without confirming also saves nothing. Notices do not verify eligibility, terms, or transactions.

Current risk notices do not implement identity checks, IP blocking, or sanctions screening. New processing requires separate explanation of data, purposes, recipients, retention, and cross-border arrangements. Buttons, page state, and local suppression records are not proof of compliance screening. Full documents remain in Docs and the footer; reading does not consent to every data use.

## III. Personal agent, conversations, and optional holdings

The personal agent uses server interfaces and a database; data is not exclusively stored in the browser. When enabled, the service processes:

- **Sign-in and account.** Public address, network, service site, challenge and expiry, signature verification, and session information. Sign-in does not authorize onchain withdrawals. Sessions identify requests; related security and rate-limit records may be server-side. Sign-out or session expiry does not automatically delete tasks or payment records.
- **Conversations and tasks.** Messages, selected vaults/templates, scope, quotes, versions, deadlines, AI outputs, revisions, delivery state, and model usage support the requested feature, conversation continuity, delivery verification, and usage accounting. Do not enter keys, seed phrases, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- **Model requests.** The current live model path uses Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5.5. Requests include the task, necessary template/vault data, onchain query context, existing plans, and recent conversations. The program does not intentionally include the signed-in address in holdings summaries, but addresses or identity you enter and other necessary context may contain such information. Complete anonymity is not guaranteed. Provider retention and use must be checked against live configuration, contracts, and current policy; no universal non-retention or no-other-use promise is made.
- **Optional holdings sharing.** Off by default and selected separately. When enabled, onchain data services query approved assets and BNB for the connected wallet. The model receives names, approximate amounts, and estimated values, without the address or exact balances in that summary. Other unlisted wallet tokens are not read by this feature. Holdings are read again when a task runs; a complete raw snapshot is not separately saved. Queries themselves still include the address, and outputs or your messages may mention holdings. Disabling future sharing does not retract requests already sent.

Recent conversations may inform later tasks. The current program uses a 30-day conversation-text window, with service processes periodically redacting expired input and response text. This does not delete all task structure, payments, credits, reconciliation, or backups. Clearing chat first removes ended conversations from display and future context; it is not immediate deletion of all server records. Unresolved payments or refunds may still appear. Live policy must verify process execution, backups, and legally required retention periods.

## IV. Credits, payments, reconciliation, and support

Using an available paid feature processes purchase/task identifiers, payer, amount, token, chain, recipient, price and terms version, quote expiry, transaction hash, operation identity and replay checks, approvals, results, credit changes, usage, and refunds to prevent duplicates and reconcile delivery.

Credit purchases use reviewed purchase functionality and wallet transactions, with purchase events public onchain. The backend verifies final events before crediting the account. If a B402/x402 route is separately enabled, authorization and settlement data may go to configured payment providers. Each route's availability and recipients must be identified before purchase or payment. Disabled features must not be presented as taking live payments.

Authorizations, unknown payments, tasks, and reconciliation records do not disappear merely because a session expires or chat is cleared, which could cause duplicate charging. They also need not all be retained forever. Live terms must specify necessary retention for service, tax, fraud prevention, and other duties.

Support providers and tools process messages, contacts, and transaction information you supply. Channels should be published before live support. Do not send keys, seed phrases, or unnecessary identity documents. Payment or support providers do not gain vault-principal withdrawal authority.

## V. Third parties, cross-border processing, and choices

Interfaces and services may send necessary requests to onchain data services, wallets/WalletConnect, asset search and market-data services such as GeckoTerminal, external font services such as Google Fonts, AI, hosting, and payment providers. Fields and recipients depend on features and configuration. Live policy must identify providers, purposes, transfer destinations, and legally required safeguards.

Addresses, transactions, balances, and holdings are public onchain and may link to other identity information. Public availability does not permit unrestricted subsequent use. External AI/MCP platforms, Operators, wallets, and protocols you choose have their own flows and policies. CRE8 remains legally responsible for its own selection, transmission, and integration.

Necessary service, security, and compliance processing require their respective legal grounds. Optional holdings sharing, analytics, and marketing must be presented separately, without preselected consent or making it a condition of existing-position exits. Documents can be read without a wallet. Not enabling the personal agent or sending a message does not trigger its model tasks. Refusing data necessary for a feature may prevent that feature.

## VI. Retention, security, and rights

Deleting browser data does not delete servers or public-chain records or revoke submitted transactions or allowances. Before removing pending records, check the original hash to avoid resubmission.

Live services should provide legally available access, correction, restriction, deletion, withdrawal of optional consent, and complaint channels, with retention obligations and exceptions. Platforms usually cannot modify or delete public-chain records, and cannot promise immediate deletion of independently held third-party records or backups.

Security must be proportionate to purpose and risk, but cannot guarantee no unauthorized access. This draft does not require identity evidence, IP, private contact information, or risk confirmations on the public chain.
