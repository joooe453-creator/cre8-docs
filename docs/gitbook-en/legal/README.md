# Legal documents and eligibility

Legal documents remain drafts that are not in effect. The service provider and markets open to live service have not been finalized. Reading, dismissing a risk notice, or connecting a wallet does not permit live funding.

| Document | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Before you use CRE8](before-you-use.md) | Summary of funds, fees, permissions, withdrawals, and AI services |
| [Draft terms of use](terms.md) | Platform, creator, and Operator roles and their rights and obligations |
| [Vault risk disclosure](risk.md) | Market, operation, fee, third-party, and exit risks |
| [Eligibility and jurisdiction restrictions](eligibility.md) | Eligibility, proposed admission policy, and existing positions |
| [Privacy notice](privacy.md) | Wallets, chat, optional holdings, credits, and payment data |

The full legal source version is 2026-10-07-draft.8. This book adapts it for product reading, removes internal and implementation wording, and does not bring the draft into effect. Live terms still require arrangements for the entity, eligibility, contacts, necessary consent, and data policy.

AI credits and fund fees are separate. See [CRE8 AI and credits](../guides/cre8-ai-credits.md) and [Fees](../product/fees.md).
