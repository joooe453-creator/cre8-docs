# Holder guide

Choose a fund whose operating scope and withdrawal conditions you understand. Funds with the same assets or similar performance figures may have different creators, AI agents, allocation caps, and third-party risks.

> Example funds and performance are simulated. They do not show real management ability and cannot accept live deposits.

## Evaluate a fund

| What to check | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Status and network | Is this an example, a test environment, or a verified live fund? Which network does it use? |
| Accounting and holding assets | What do you deposit? What can it buy? Are there price or depeg risks? |
| Designated markets | Which protocols receive assets? How do receipts turn back into their underlying assets? |
| Creator and AI agent | Who sets the plan? Which AI agent acts on its own? Who may pause the fund? |
| Allocation caps | What is the maximum allocation per asset? Are changes pending? |
| Fees | Each deposit pays a 1% deposit fee. What is the total performance fee (CRE8 10% + creator)? What counts as chargeable profit? What other costs apply, such as gas, DEX fees, and slippage? |
| Performance data | Is it simulated, estimated, or actual history? What period is covered, and when was it updated? |
| Withdrawals | What assets can you receive? What happens if liquidity or valuation fails? |

## What you receive

Depositing the accounting asset gives you shares recorded by the fund. Under the rules, they give you your part of the fund's asset value and of withdrawal results. Gains and losses both affect the share price.

Shares are nontransferable and do not protect principal. Each holding address has its own net-contribution cost basis for the performance fee charged when you withdraw. See [Fees](../product/fees.md).

## Who acts after your deposit

The AI agent uses its own signer within the fund's designated scope. Holders do not approve every later trade or supply operation.

A single holder cannot replace the fund's AI agent. The creator can disable or replace the AI agent through the applicable governance process and delay. Revoking a token allowance in your wallet affects future access to assets in that wallet; it does not revoke the AI agent's authority over assets already deposited in the fund.

The AI agent role does not grant special authority to withdraw for you. If you separately authorize someone to handle your fund shares, check that authorization on its own.

## What to monitor

Check holdings, allocations and caps, data timestamps, whether deposits are paused and why, and announced changes that are not yet effective. When data is unavailable, do not treat blanks, stale values, or example charts as the current asset position.

Live performance records have not been established. Simulated figures only illustrate how to read the interface. See [Share price and performance](../product/nav-and-performance.md).
