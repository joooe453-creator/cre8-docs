# CRE8 AI and credits

CRE8 AI helps explore and compare vaults, design proposals, and review strategies. You must review its output. It does not hold your private keys, and buying credits does not grant it authority over your funds.

CRE8 AI conversations and proposal generation use **Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5.5**. The model supports vault comparisons, vault design, and strategy reviews; routine execution of approved strategies follows fixed rules without calling the chat model.

> **Draft pricing and features | 2026-10-07:** These prices appear in the current configuration and launch design. Live payment deployment and acceptance checks are incomplete. This page does not promise that purchases are available now. Once open, the purchase and task-confirmation screens determine the price, asset, credits, and conditions.

## Free questions and AI credits

The current example configuration provides 5 free questions per wallet in a seven-day window beginning with the first question. It does not reset every Monday. Check the account screen for the live allowance and reset time. Free access is also subject to service availability and the overall subsidy budget.

After free questions are used, credits let you continue asking. Comparisons, design proposals, and strategy reviews use credits. Credits are CRE8 AI service units in your account: they cannot be transferred or withdrawn and are not vault shares. If your balance is insufficient, add credits or use vault operations directly.

## Draft credit-pack prices

The purchase design uses designated USDT on BNB Smart Chain. Same-name tokens, USDT on other networks, and test tokens are not interchangeable. Before a live purchase, verify the network and full asset address on screen.

| Draft price | Credits received | Extra credits relative to the basic pack |
|---|---|---|
| 1 USDT | 100 credits | — |
| 5 USDT | 525 credits | 5% |
| 20 USDT | 2,200 credits | 10% |

Prices exclude BNB gas. Extra credits are not investment yield, and credits cannot be redeemed for the USDT shown. Expiry, refunds, and treatment of unused credits if the service stops must be specified in live purchase terms. Perpetual validity or refunds on demand are not promised.

## How credits are charged

| Use | Current draft maximum per run | When charged |
|---|---|---|
| A question after the free allowance | Up to 10 credits | After the answer is delivered |
| Vault comparison | Up to 60 credits | After delivery |
| Vault design | Up to 60 credits | After the proposal is delivered |
| Strategy review | Up to 60 credits | After delivery |
| Task revision | The applicable task maximum per run | After the revision is delivered, charged separately by usage |
| Checking an existing task's status or viewing delivered results | No credits charged | — |

The current mode charges actual AI usage, rounded up, without exceeding the displayed maximum for that run. “Up to 10 credits” is not a fixed charge of 10 per question. “Up to 60 credits” is not an all-inclusive price for every later revision. If a fixed-charge mode is introduced, the confirmation screen should identify it.

Before starting, your balance is checked against the maximum. Credits are deducted only after delivery; no delivery means no charge. One wallet may have only one credit-funded request running at a time. After delivery, view credits spent and the remaining balance. Included revision count, scope, and deadline follow the confirmation screen; a credit balance does not imply unlimited revisions.

A delivered proposal remains a delivered service even if you do not create a vault. It is not automatically free in that case. For problems, keep the task identifier and use the officially published support channel.

## How to purchase once open

1. Connect your wallet, sign in to CRE8 AI, and verify the account.
2. Select a pack and review price, credits, network, asset, payment information, and purchase terms.
3. Confirm any necessary token approval and the purchase transaction. They are separate steps; approval alone is not a purchase.
4. Wait for final confirmation of the original purchase. Credits enter the original purchasing wallet's account only after service verification.
5. Check the purchase record and balance before starting a question or task.

If a purchase is pending, you close the page, or the screen times out, query the original order and transaction before paying again. The system attempts to recover the purchase, but automatic lookup has time and data limits. If credits remain missing, keep the order and hash for support reconciliation. Rejection, failure, and an unknown outcome are different states.

## AI credits and vault fees are separate

Credits pay for conversations and planning services, not entry fees, fund-operation fees, performance fees, protocol costs, or gas. Payment, sign-in, and accepting a proposal do not grant Operator authority. Creation, approvals, deposits, and exits retain their own confirmations.

Approved strategies execute continuously using time, prices, and fixed rules. Routine execution does not call the chat model or consume AI credits, but execution services, market data, protocols, and networks still have costs. Exhausted credits should not block direct queries, deposits, exits, or authority management; those operations still require valid deployment, eligibility, and vault conditions.

## Data and verification

Entering chat or the interface preview shows a notice that AI can make mistakes. Press “Got it” to continue. There is no X; Escape returns to the previous page, or home if there is none. Only checking “Don’t show this again” and pressing “Got it” saves the local suppression choice. This is not legal acceptance, payment authorization, or consent to share holdings. Holdings sharing is off by default and optional. AI output may be wrong, incomplete, or outdated. Verify important information, assets, fees, and transaction terms yourself. Chat uses server-side data and AI services; it is not entirely local or anonymous.

Do not enter private keys, seed phrases, or unnecessary sensitive data. See [Privacy notice](../legal/privacy.md), [Agent guide](agents.md), [Vault fees](../product/fees.md), and [Troubleshooting](../reference/troubleshooting.md).
